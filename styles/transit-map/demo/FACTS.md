Notes on definitions:

- **19 lines** is Guangzhou Metro's own count: Lines 1–14, 18, 21, 22, the Guangfo Line and the APM. Trams (3 lines, 26.3 km) and intercity lines (6 lines) are counted separately and are not drawn.
- **Route length** includes the Guangfo Line's Foshan section and Line 7's Shunde section (the 2010 figure already includes the whole first Guangfo section; the year-to-year differences match the section lengths). Each landing names its date on the counter.
- **Line 11 and 21.** Line 21 opened to Yuancun in 2019; in October–December 2024 its Tianhe Park–Yuancun stretch was handed over to the Line 11 ring (news.dayoo.com/guangzhou/202409/29/139995_54725785.htm). The map shows today's lines: Line 21 is drawn to Tianhe Park in 2019, and Tianhe Park–Yuancun is drawn with the ring in 2024.
- **Old Line 2.** The 2002–2005 Line 2 (Sanyuanli–Wanshengwei) was split in 2010: Changgang–Wanshengwei became part of Line 8. Track is drawn in the colour of the line that runs on it today.
- **Stations opened later on an open line** are drawn with their line: Airport South is Line 3's 2010 terminus (missing from OpenStreetMap, added by `tools/prep.py`); Chigang (Line 12, 2026-02-13), Zhongda Nanmen (Line 10, 2025-12-29) and Guangzhou East on Line 11 (2025-09-29) appear with their lines.
- **Station names** are today's: the 1997 terminus was written 西朗 and is now 西塱 (Xilang); the film does not print it in the opening.

## Map

- Network: OpenStreetMap route relations (`neo/geo/routes.py`, network 广州地铁 / 广佛, fetched 2026-11-13), © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Station order, line topology and the published line colours (OSM `colour` tags) are kept; the geometry is our own octilinear schematic (`tools/prep.py`), not the operator's map.
- Water: OpenStreetMap waterways (`neo/geo/osm.py`), the named Pearl River channels only; drawn as schematic bands. `tools/prep.py` checks, for every pair of neighbouring stations, that the schematic line crosses the same channel as the real track (five accepted exceptions are listed in `WATER_OK`).
- No borders are drawn (DIRECTOR.md §13); the view is the city region only.
