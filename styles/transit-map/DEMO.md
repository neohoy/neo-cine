# Transit Map — our demo

One example among many. Don't reuse its story, arc, shots, props or timings.

Demo: *The River and the Ring* 《江与环》 (54 s) · `transit-map.mp4` · source in [`demo/`](demo/) · 中文版：[DEMO.zh-CN.md](DEMO.zh-CN.md)

Guangzhou Metro from its first five stations in 1997 to 19 lines and 779.9 km in 2025, on a printed schematic. **An unofficial fan film, not affiliated with Guangzhou Metro Group or any operator**: no operator logo, lettering or official map; the numbered discs are generic bullets; line colours, numbers and station names are public facts. The map data is OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0); the schematic is our own drawing. Trains are illustrative. Every date and figure on screen is sourced in [`demo/FACTS.md`](demo/FACTS.md). The style is adapted from Lemo-Opuscar's Transit Map (MIT, © 2026 LemoLab).

## Story & structure

Logline: a river cuts the city in three; the network learns to cross it, reaches the far districts, and closes a ring.

- **The river (0–8 s).** A bare sheet: only the Pearl River's channels and the printed title 江与环 / The River and the Ring. In 1997 the first tip leaves Xilang, drags north through five stations and dives under the river at Baietan to Huangsha; a callout prints "1997.6.28 · 1 号线首段 · 5 站".
- **The old city (6–14 s).** Line 1 reaches Guangzhou East (1999), then 2002–2010 knot the old city: Lines 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, the Guangfo Line and the APM, crossing the front and back channels again and again. The year counter spins through the years whose dates are not officially sourced and lands on 2010 with 236 km.
- **The reach (14–26 s).** The camera rises until the sheet's edge and the table show. Lines 6, 7, 9, 13, 14, 21, Line 4 to Nansha and Line 18 throw the network to the far districts (Huadu, Conghua, Zengcheng, Huangpu, Nansha); the counter lands 2017 (13 lines, 390.6 km) and 2020 (531.1 km); a callout prints Line 18's 160 km/h. 2022–2024 fill in the south and east.
- **The ring (26–34 s).** Back down over the city. The network goes under a paper veil and Line 11 draws the 2024 ring around the core in one stroke; every interchange it passes bounces; the counter lands 17 lines, 705.1 km as the ring closes.
- **Full (34–47 s).** 2025: Lines 10 and 12, then 13 and 14, then 22 complete the core (751.1 km, then 19 lines and 779.9 km). A low sweep over Tiyu Xilu, Zhujiang New Town and Canton Tower with trains running everywhere; the counter adds the national single-day record, 14.09 million trips on 2025-12-31.
- **The whole (47–54 s).** The camera rises to the whole network; bullets reappear at the line ends; the end block prints in the sheet's right margin.

Native moves spent: the dragged line (1997), the tunnel under the water (Baietan), levels of detail (old city), rise to the sheet edge (the reach), the featured line under a veil (the ring), the springing interchange (throughout, strongest on the ring), the living network (from 1997; the sweep), the light counter (throughout).

## Score structure

No fixed tempo: the melody comes from the picture. Every `pass` event (a tip passing a station) plays one note in that line's voice; D major pentatonic, climbing along the line and turning back at the top (Line 11's ring climbs over two octaves). Voices: marimba (Lines 1, 8, 14), glass bell (2, 10, 18), kalimba (3, 9, 21), harp (4, 11, Guangfo), vibraphone (5, 12), nylon string (6, 13), music box (7, 22, APM).

| Section | Time | Palette |
|---|---|---|
| River | 0–6.4 | a glass bell at 0.3; paper and water hiss; D pad; Line 1's marimba notes; soft click at each corner, pop at Huangsha |
| Old city | 6.4–14.2 | Bm pad, bass on the change; a cloud of notes as five lines grow at once; ticks for the 236 km landing |
| Reach | 14.2–27.3 | G, then A; long lines play long rising phrases; terminus tocks at the far ends; paper wipe for the Line 18 callout |
| Ring | 27.3–32.6 | D pad; a filtered riser under the ring; harp notes at each of its 30 stations; at the close a harp D-major-add9 spread and a glass bell |
| Full | 33.9–40.4 | Bm, G; the 2025 lines; landings |
| Sweep | 40.4–46.6 | A pad, distant rail tocks; 0.6 s of near-silence, then a glass-bell octave and low D for the record |
| End | 47–54 | D add9 pad; a slow harp spread from 48.6; a last bell; 0.5 s fade |

Mix (`mix.py`): melody, pad, effects and a short bright room reverb, each bus peak-tamed (13 dB crest), then `neo/audio/master.py` (−14 LUFS, true peak −2 dBTP); `neo/audio/cues.py` reports 0 cues off the picture (all 1,059 sounds are driven by the picture's events).

## Palette & props

- Paper #F4F1EA under the raking light on a near-black table; ink #1B1C1E; water #BCD6EA with #8FB5D6 edges; water labels #5F88AD.
- Line colours: Guangzhou Metro's published ones from OpenStreetMap's colour tags (1 #F3D03E, 2 #00629B, 3 #ECA154, 4 #00843D, 5 #C5003E, 6 #80225F, 7 #97D700, 8 #008C95, 9 #71CC98, 10 #7389B2, 11 #FAC525, 12 #435428, 13 #8E8C13, 14 #81312F, 18 #0047BA, 21 #201747, 22 #CD5228, Guangfo #C4D600, APM #00B5E2). Lines 1 and 11 are both yellow: the veil separates them in the ring.
- The schematic: 19 lines, 318 stations, 117 skeleton nodes, generated by `tools/prep.py` from OpenStreetMap and pinned by hand in the core; the Pearl River's west, front and back channels, the Huangpu channel down to the estuary, and the Shawan waterway.
- Props: the printed title block west of Baietan; two callouts (1997, Line 18); the light counter card (year, lines, route length, record); the end block in the right margin.

## End card

Printed in the sheet's right margin in the final wide view, beside the whole network: 江与环 / The River and the Ring; NEO-Cine · 地铁线路图 / NEO-Cine · Transit Map. Held 5.3 s. The fan-film note, "trains illustrative, schematic drawn by us", the OpenStreetMap credit (ODbL) and "adapted from Lemo-Opuscar's Transit Map (MIT)" are in [`demo/CREDITS`](demo/CREDITS) and at the top of this file, not on screen: a library demo signs off with NEO-Cine only. The NEO-Cine sign-off belongs to this library's demo only; a user's film carries no NEO-Cine credit and no copy of this card.

## Build notes

```
demo/
  build.sh       one command, versioned: sh styles/transit-map/demo/build.sh v001 (RENDER=remote for the render desktop)
  index.html     two canvases (WebGL plane, 2D overlay), fonts, scripts
  net.js         generated: the Guangzhou schematic (stations, 19 lines, water bands, the estuary)
  gl.js          makePlane(): calm paper, light, sheet edge, depth of field, vignette
  film.js        the generic engine (no network, no story)
  timeline.js    this film: facts (FX), phases, camera, labels per shot, veil and ring, title, callouts, end block,
                 trains, the counter card, cues
  mix.py         the score and effects, synthesized; cues and master checks -> out/mix.wav
  data/          metro.json (OSM routes) and water.json (the named channels), both ODbL
  tools/         prep.py (OSM -> octilinear schematic -> net.js, with layout and water checks; --preview out.png
                 draws a quick PNG), fonts.sh (pinned font downloads), subset.py (Noto Sans SC subset)
  FACTS.md, CREDITS
```

- Build: `sh styles/transit-map/demo/build.sh v001` (refuses an existing version). Steps: OSM data if missing (`neo/geo/routes.py`, `neo/geo/osm.py`), `tools/prep.py` if `net.js` is missing, fonts and subset, `events.json`, `mix.py`, picture (local `core/render/video.mjs` with 3 workers, or `RENDER=remote` with `neo/render/remote.sh` and 4 workers), `mux.sh` with `LEMO_COLOR=bt709` and grain 1, `readcheck.mjs`, poster (43.6 s) and style frame (33.5 s).
- Measured: the mix −14.0 LUFS before mux, true peak −2.0 dB, loudness range about 7.4 LU (linear master); `readcheck.mjs` passes on every text. Frames take 40–400 ms on an Apple-silicon Mac; the 1,296 frames render on the desktop's RTX 4060 Ti in about 72 s (222 s for v003, where one worker's page timed out once and was reopened); the whole `build.sh` takes 3.5–6 minutes. The film measures −13.8 LUFS after mux.
- Editing the map: change `MOVE`, `CORNER`, `FORCE_NODES` or `RIVERS` in `tools/prep.py`, run it (it prints stations too close and every river crossing that disagrees with the real track), then rebuild. `--preview` writes a flat PNG of the layout with rivers.
- Hooks this film defines: `LABEL_PREF`, `labelOnly`/`forceLabel` (names per shot), `roundelPos` as a function (bullets at the line ends at the end), `roundelAlpha`, `veil`/`stationDim`/`highlight` (the ring), `waterLabels`, `furniture` (title, end block), `annotations` (callouts), `trains` (`service`), `overlay` (counter), `cues`, `init`.
- Demo-specific pitfalls: Line 11 is a loop (its last station takes the path's end); Lines 1 and 11 share a yellow; OpenStreetMap misses Line 3's Airport South (added by `prep.py`); the old Line 2 became Lines 2 and 8 in 2010 and Line 21's Tianhe Park–Yuancun stretch became part of Line 11 in 2024 (track is drawn in today's colours, see FACTS.md); year dates known only from encyclopaedias make the year counter spin instead of land.
