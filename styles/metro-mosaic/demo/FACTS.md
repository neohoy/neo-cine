Scope, on purpose: the 2026 figure is the city's **urban rail** total ("城市轨道交通"), as the Transport Bureau words it, and is labelled that way on screen. It very probably includes the Longhua tram (about 11.7 km); the 2011–2020 figures do not (the tram opened in 2017, and the 2020 figure is "深圳地铁全网里程（含4号线）"). The plaques show each figure as published and never interpolate between them: a plaque changes only by a new plaque being hung over it.

## Checked and not used

- First-day length: 21.3 km (szmc.net 2024-12-27, "线路全长21.3公里，共设车站20座") vs 21.866 km (sz.gov.cn 深圳故事 2022-12-18); both are for the whole first phase, including 皇岗 which opened in 2007. Not shown.
- 2011: the statistical bulletin gives 177.00 km ("轨道交通线路长度177.00公里") against 178 km in the operator's chronicle and the press. 178 used.
- 2020: the statistical bulletin gives 422.6 km and 12 lines (it counts the tram); 411 km and 11 lines used, as the metro figure of the opening day.
- 2022: 559 km incl. tram (bulletin and sznews.com), 516.7 km for the Shenzhen Metro Group's own lines. Not shown.
- Line count today: 18 (2025 statistical bulletin, "运营线路18条") vs 20 (Special Zone Daily 2025-09). Conflicting, so no line count is shown for 2026.
- Ridership: record day 13.05 million (2025-12-31, sz.gov.cn), August 2026 monthly total above Shanghai and Beijing (Special Zone Daily 2026-09-16). Not used.
- Line 10's 岗厦北 platform and Line 7's 福临 station: opening dates not found; 福临 is not in the current official table and is not drawn.
