# Scrapbook Song — our demo

One example among many. Don't reuse its story, arc, shots, props or timings.

Demo: *Wind Through Hsinchu* 《风吹新竹》 (48.5 s) · `scrapbook-song.mp4` · source in [`demo/`](demo/) · 中文版：[DEMO.zh-CN.md](DEMO.zh-CN.md)

Song: 《风吹新竹》, words, music and vocals by hoy neo (2026). The demo is a 48.5 s excerpt of the hand-drawn music video made for the song: the same drawings and timelines, regenerated by the same Python, now this style's kit ([`kit/`](kit/)), and played by this library's page contract. The demo folder is itself a project made with the kit, the way a user's film is made ([`PRODUCTION.md`](PRODUCTION.md)). The Hsinchu landmarks and foods are drawn from their general shape; nothing here is an official depiction of a place.

## Story & structure

The song: a child who grew up in Hsinchu, the "windy city", is blown by the wind to the sea, to many cities, and back home. The demo takes the bridge and the final chorus (song time 146.64–190.60) and adds an end card.

- **Apart (0–9.5 s).** Two friends in two cities, a snowy night and a sunny palm street, each looking at a phone; a paper plane crosses the jagged seam. A wind band; then the suitcase seen from above, and home drops in on the beats: rice noodles, persimmons, a kite, the old ticket, a photo, a bamboo leaf.
- **The gust (9.5–17.8 s).** A grey foreign street; the grown-up walks, a gust carries one bamboo leaf from home past them and they stop. Then a quiet page: holding a conch to the ear, "I still hear the waves of that year" written down a column, the 风城 seal on the downbeat.
- **The chorus (17.8–34 s).** A pink sunburst close-up in the gust, the burst on the kick, 风吹新竹 slammed, 啊 in a bubble, two labels; a wind band to the coast, the scooter ride from the East Gate to the sea, the ticket, the ribbon; nightfall, five buildings thump up, the title slammed across the sky and blown away, the walker with a suitcase, windows lighting in clusters on the half-beats, a hanging tag and a ribbon.
- **Home (34–44 s).** The wind blows the windows dark; the camera dives into the last lit one; inside, soup and persimmons, the line writes itself and 家 (home) drops on the snare. Then the scrapbook page: polaroid of the two at the sea, tape, ticket, the hand-written "hoy neo", the 风吹新竹 seal. A pink wind band.
- **End card (44–48.5 s).** NEO-Cine slammed letter by letter, the 手账歌谣 seal, the song fading under it.

Native moves spent: things drop into a container (suitcase), split screen, a column of type on a quiet page, the burst close-up, the scooter on its curve, passing lights up (windows), push into the last lit window, the seal on the beat, the photo on the scrapbook page, blow-off (the slammed title), wind bands throughout.

## Score structure

The song itself, 99.4 BPM, 4/4. The excerpt starts at the bridge (the quietest part of the song) and rises into the final chorus, so the cut's biggest contrast is built in. Fade in 0.25 s, fade out over the last 2.6 s (the song's outro continues under the end card). No sound is added. `mix.py` levels it with one gain to −14 LUFS (the song arrives at −14.6 LUFS; the limiter works 0.1 % of the time) and checks that the 13 picture hits (6 suitcase drops, 风城 seal, burst, sparks, 家, 风吹新竹 seal, end slam, end seal) sit on the song's beats or drum hits: largest offset 8 ms.

## Palette & props

Paper `#F3EDE0`, ink `#191919`, pink `#E8457A`, teal `#3FB8A4`, yellow `#F5D03B`, red `#C8352E`, night `#1E2A78`, orange `#F08A3C`, bamboo `#3E8E5A`, buff `#F2D08B`. Ground order: night/orange split → paper → grey-blue → paper → pink → teal → night → yellow → paper → paper. The protagonist: black bob, white shirt or orange coat, **yellow scarf** (the colour of the wind and the light); the friend: long hair, a pink clip. The wind always blows right to left. Props from the lyrics and from Hsinchu: rice-noodle bundles, persimmons, a kite, the old ticket "东门城 → 海岸", the heart-shaped stone weir, fiddler crabs, the East Gate, meatball rice-noodle soup.

## End card

Paper, wind curls, a bamboo leaf; **NEO-Cine** slammed letter by letter in the display face on 190.82, the red seal **手账歌谣** (the style's name) on 192.03. Nothing else. The song's title and its singer stay where the original video put them: on the song's own title page (the scrapbook page, shot 9), as information about the work. A user's film carries no NEO-Cine credit and no copy of this card.

## Build notes

```
demo/                       a Scrapbook Song project, like any user's film
  mv.json                   title, song excerpt (audio_t0 146.0), range 146.64–195.20, the scene table, hits, pass_texts, poster
  audio/song-excerpt.mp3    the 50 s of the song the film uses (in git)
  data/                     lyrics.json (48 lines, times), audiomap.json (the original video's beat analysis, see CREDITS)
  scripts/scenes/           full_s18, full_s19, build_f1–f4 (the original video's scenes), end_card
  scripts/assets/           hd_people, props2, city (this song's drawings), songlib (its props and this cut's page numbers)
  build.sh                  sh styles/scrapbook-song/demo/build.sh v002 (RENDER=remote for the GPU desktop)
```

`build.sh` runs the kit's `tools/make.sh` with the demo as the project and writes the film and poster next to the style (`OUTDIR`): scenes → fonts (the subset follows the scenes' text) → events → `tools/mix.py` (the excerpt, fades, cue check, −14 LUFS) → render → mux → readcheck, lyriccheck → poster (38.5 s); then the style frame (21.7 s) and the gallery copies when they are missing. Rendering 1165 frames on the desktop (RTX 4060 Ti, 4 workers) takes about 100 s plus encoding.

- **The original climax scenes** (`build_f1`–`f4`) write their timelines by hand instead of through `Scene`, so their beat hits are listed in `mv.json` `hits`; `songlib.tag` maps their original "n / 6" page numbers to this cut.
- **The analysis** in `data/audiomap.json` is the one the original video was timed against (librosa); a new film gets its own from `tools/analyze.py`, which on this song matches the original downbeats and the waveform onsets to a few milliseconds.
- **Changes from the original video:** page numbers renumbered for this cut (01–09); the scrapbook page held 0.7 s longer so the 风吹新竹 seal is on screen 2.8 s; the end card is new. Everything else is generated from the original code unchanged (checked: the same SVG and timelines).
- **Checks (v001):** `readcheck` 0 BAD (17 timed; page numbers, the in-transit ticket and the 风城 stamp listed as passing, reasons in `mv.json` `notes`); `lyriccheck` 12 lines, 0 failures — every line is complete by the end of its singing — with notes inherited from the original timing: lines 41 and 46 stay complete only 0.6 / 0.5 s before the cut, line 43's ribbon only 0.12 s, line 47 (家) leaves 2 s before its held last note ends, and most lines hold less than max(1.8 s, sung + 0.6 s); cue check 13 hits, largest offset 8 ms; `mux.sh` −13.9 LUFS; no black frames.
- **Pitfall of this demo:** the f4 push-in scales a group 31×; the displacement filter on it uses user-space bounds (`f4-wobZ`), or the boil would grow with the zoom.
